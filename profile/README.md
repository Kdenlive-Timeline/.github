## Core Kdenlive Features

- **Timeline Editing:** Place clips you shot or were allowed to use on a timeline, trim the ends, and arrange them in the order the story needs.
- **Separate Tracks:** Keep picture, voice, and music on different tracks so a level change does not force you to recut the image.
- **Project File:** Save the edit as a project that points at your media. The export is a new video file, not a replacement for the camera originals.
- **Preview and Export:** Watch the cut inside the editor, then export a file you can open in an ordinary player before you delete anything.

---

## About Kdenlive

Kdenlive is a video editor for footage you already have. A phone clip, a screen recording you made, or an interview you conducted can go on the timeline. It does not download films, and it does not remove copy protection from a disc. If the only way to get a picture is to break a lock, this is the wrong tool.

The first project should be short. Two clips and one cut teach the window faster than an hour of unsorted media. Save the project in the same folder as the clips so the paths stay obvious next week. If you move the videos and do not relink them, the timeline goes black even though the files still exist.

Export when the cut is right. Keep the camera files until you have watched the export and you know you will not need a different ending. Music under the picture has to be music you may use. A commercial track you do not have rights to is not a soundtrack just because it fits.

## Tool Benefits

- **Originals Stay:** The project points at your files. Deleting those files to "save space" breaks the edit.
- **One Clear Deliverable:** The exported file is what you send. The project is what you reopen.
- **A Small First Cut:** One or two shots are enough to prove playback, audio, and export before a longer piece.

## Getting Started with Kdenlive

Prerequisites: Clips you may edit, stored in a folder with room for an export.

1. **Create a Project:** Save it beside the media, not in a temporary download folder.
2. **Add Two Clips:** Put them on the timeline and trim the ends you do not want.
3. **Play the Cut:** Listen for a missing track or a muted voice before you export.
4. **Export and Watch:** Open the new file outside Kdenlive. Keep the originals until that viewing is done.

## Professional & Personal Use Cases

- **A Personal Recap:** Cut a holiday or a talk you recorded into a length someone will actually watch.

- **A Class Project:** Edit material you have the right to use, and keep the project file with the clips.
